Domain-System Official Documentation (v2.7.0)

A full-featured, ultra-lightweight (~30 MB on disk & ~5 MB of RAM), fast platform in pure PHP 8.2.

👋 Hello! An honest call to the developer community

Domain System was built by a self-taught developer with a clear goal: reclaim the speed, simplicity, and efficiency of modern PHP without relying on bloated multi-gigabyte frameworks or endless plugin mazes.

Building mostly on my own, I use AI models as relentless daily code auditing partners (with over 650 automated tests in PHPUnit). However, nothing replaces the real-world experience, intuition, and sanity checks of experienced human engineers.

I warmly invite you to test, audit the code, try to break it, and help us make it even more stable, secure, and fast!
